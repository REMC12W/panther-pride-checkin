Panther Pride Center terms of use

Updated October 26, 2026

The Panther Pride Center check-in app is provided by Parchment School District, with support from Kalamazoo RESA, for use in the Parchment Middle School Pride Center. Using it means you agree to these terms.

Who may use it

  • Students use the check-in iPad in the Pride Center, with staff nearby. They do not need an account and do not agree to anything by checking in.
  • Staff sign in with their school Google account. Only accounts the principal has approved can open the dashboards or sign in a Pride Center iPad. Access can be changed or removed by the principal at any time.

Using it responsibly

  • Use the app only for Pride Center work. Check-in records, incident reports, and student names are confidential school records.
  • Do not share your sign-in, leave a signed-in dashboard open where others can see it, or copy student information outside district systems.
  • Sign a Pride Center iPad in only on a device the school manages, and sign it out if it leaves the room.
  • Follow district policy on student records and the privacy notice for this app.

What the app is and isn't

The app helps staff notice and respond. It is not a medical, counseling, or emergency tool and does not replace a staff member's judgment. A student who needs help right away should get it from an adult, not from the app.

The app is provided as is. The district and Kalamazoo RESA keep it running in good faith but do not promise it will always be available or error free. If it is offline, check-ins are kept on the iPad and sent when it reconnects.

Your data

Everything the app records belongs to Parchment School District and stays in its Google Workspace. See the privacy notice for what is recorded and who can see it.

Changes

The district may update the app or these terms. The date at the top shows when they last changed.

Questions

Contact Parchment Middle School, or the Kalamazoo RESA Educator Supports team at ben.tomlinson@kresa.org.
