Panther Pride Center privacy notice

Updated October 26, 2026

The Panther Pride Center check-in app is a school tool. Students use it on a Pride Center iPad to say how they are feeling when they arrive and when they leave. Pride Center staff and the principal use it to see who needs support. This page explains what the app records, where it is kept, and who can see it.

What the app records about students

When a student checks in, the app records:

  • the student's name and student ID, picked from the school's roster list, and their grade
  • the date and time of the visit
  • why they came (on their own or sent by staff, a scheduled break, a reset, or a basic need)
  • the mood color and feeling word they picked, and the station they chose
  • a basic need they asked for, such as a snack or school supplies
  • when they checked out, how they felt leaving, and what helped

If a student reports that something happened to them, the app also records what happened, where, when, and a short description in their own words, along with any follow-up a staff member adds.

The app does not record a student's photo, location, voice, or anything typed on a personal device. Students never sign in to a Google account or any other account.

What the app records about staff

Staff sign in with their school Google account. The app keeps the staff member's email address, which pages they are allowed to open, and a note of who reviewed an incident report or signed in a Pride Center iPad. Dashboards a principal builds are saved under that principal's email address.

Where the information is kept

Everything is saved in a Google Sheet owned by Parchment School District and its support partner, Kalamazoo RESA, inside their Google Workspace. The app has no separate database or company behind it. The web pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub serves the pages but never receives the data.

The Pride Center iPad keeps a copy of recent check-ins so nothing is lost if the Wi-Fi drops. That copy stays on the iPad and is sent to the Google Sheet as soon as it reconnects.

Who can see it

  • Students see only their own screens while checking in. The iPad does not show other students' names or visits.
  • Pride Center staff the principal has listed can open the staff dashboard, which shows the current day's visits and incident reports so they can respond.
  • The principal and people the principal lists can open the principal dashboard, which shows trends over time. It does not show the text of incident reports or staff notes.
  • No one else. There is no public view. Every page except the student iPad requires a school Google sign-in, and the school decides who is on the list.

Sign in with Google

The app uses Google Sign-In only to confirm who a staff member is. It receives the staff member's name, email address, and Google Workspace domain. It does not read email, files, calendars, or anything else in the Google account, and it asks for no other permissions. Google's own handling of sign-in is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies, tracking, and selling

The app uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind. It sets no tracking cookies. It uses a small amount of storage on the device only to remember a staff sign-in and to hold check-ins while offline. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than running the Pride Center.

The pages load the Raleway font from Google Fonts. That request tells Google Fonts the device's internet address, as any web font does, and nothing else.

Student privacy laws

Check-in records are school records. They are handled under the district's student records policy and the federal Family Educational Rights and Parents' Privacy Act (FERPA). The app collects no information from students over the internet and asks students for no personal information beyond picking their own name, so it does not collect personal information in the sense of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Keeping and deleting records

Records stay in the Google Sheet until the district removes them. Parents and guardians can ask to see their child's records, and the district can correct or delete them, by contacting the school office. Pride Center staff can delete a record directly in the Google Sheet.
